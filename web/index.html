    How to play

    1. Move the master controller. Push right (↑ or W) to accelerate, pull left (↓ or S) to brake. Space returns to neutral, H holds the speed. In neutral and hold the inverter keeps running, so the sound continues.
    2. Drag the line on the ruler to jump to any speed, then stop there and listen.
    3. Click a segment on the ruler to change its modulation, carrier frequency or pulse count, and press Listen to hear it. Drag the boundary between two segments to change the speed range.
    4. Take the motor apart (bottom left of the motor, or E): drag each part out along its path and click it to see what it does. The train waits meanwhile.

    Power table (A) options

    The fundamental voltage rises with speed along V/f: at the full modulation speed the reference amplitude is 1, at the full voltage speed the output equals a square wave. The dashed lines on the ruler are these two speeds.
