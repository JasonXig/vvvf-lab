How to play

  1. Move the master controller: push right (or press ↑/W) to start accelerating, pull left (↓/S) to brake. Space returns to neutral N, H holds the speed. In neutral and hold the inverter keeps running, so the sound continues.
  2. Drag the white vertical line on the ruler (or press and drag anywhere on the ruler) to jump to any speed, then stop there and listen.
  3. Click a segment on the ruler to change its modulation kind, carrier frequency or pulse count, and press "Listen" to hear it right away; drag the boundary between two segments to change the speed range.

Power table (A) options

The fundamental voltage rises with speed along V/f: at the "full modulation" speed the reference amplitude is 1, at the "full voltage" speed the output equals a square wave. The dashed lines on the ruler are these two speeds.
